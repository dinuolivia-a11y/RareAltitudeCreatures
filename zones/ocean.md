# Ocean – 10 Rare Creatures (by Depth)

1. **Bathydevius caudactylus** (“mystery mollusc”) – Swimming nudibranch with large hood and paddle-like tail. Bioluminescent. Midnight zone.
2. **Colossal squid** (*Mesonychoteuthis hamiltoni*) – Rarely filmed alive. Juvenile filmed in natural habitat in 2025.
3. **Fangtooth fish** (*Anoplogaster cornuta*) – Deep-sea specialist with huge teeth. Twilight and midnight zones.
4. Carnivorous “death-ball” sponges (*Chondrocladia*) – Covered in tiny hooks to catch prey. Abyssal depths.
5. **Zombie worms** (*Osedax*) – No mouth; symbiotic bacteria digest whale bones. Deep seafloor.
6. New iridescent scale worms – Recently observed in Southern Ocean trenches.
7. “Loki” sea cucumber (*Peniagone* sp.) – Horned appendages, recently discovered in deep canyons.
8. New amphipod superfamily *Mirabestioidea* – Entirely new branch of life from the Clarion-Clipperton Zone.
9. Snailfish filmed at record depths (>8000 m) – Soft-bodied fish adapted to extreme pressure.
10. Various new gossamer worms (*Tomopteris*) and glass squids of the mesopelagic zone.

## Notes
The deep sea is the least explored habitat on Earth. Only a small fraction of marine species have been described.
