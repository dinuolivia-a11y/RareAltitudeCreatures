# Ocean – Rare Creatures (by depth)

Solo specie con nome scientifico preciso e documentazione affidabile. Organizzate per profondità.

## Abyssal / Hadal
1. **Ferreiraella populi**  
   Deep-sea chiton discovered in 2024 in the Izu-Ogasawara Trench (~5500 m). Named after a public vote.

2. **Gigantactis paresca**  
   Deep-sea anglerfish described in 2024 from the Clarion-Clipperton Zone. Unique double lure.

3. **Careproctus colliculi**  
   Abyssal snailfish (bumpy snailfish) described in 2025 from the eastern Pacific (~3200–4100 m).

4. **Bathynomus tuebingoborikensis**  
   Giant isopod (~32 cm) described in 2025. One of the largest known isopods.

## Hydrothermal / Special habitats
5. **Apotectonia senckenbergae**  
   Amphipod described from a mussel bed at the Galápagos Rift hydrothermal vents (~2600 m).
