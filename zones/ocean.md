# Ocean – Rare Creatures by Depth Layer

Organized from shallower to deepest (Hadal) zones. Focus on obscure or recently discovered species.

## Mesopelagic / Twilight Zone (~200–1000 m)
- **Bathydevius caudactylus** (“mystery mollusc”) – Swimming nudibranch with a large hood and paddle-like tail. Bioluminescent. Lives in the midnight zone.
- Various new gossamer worms (*Tomopteris*) and glass squids.

## Bathypelagic / Midnight Zone (~1000–4000 m)
- **Colossal squid** (*Mesonychoteuthis hamiltoni*) – Rarely filmed alive. Juvenile filmed in natural habitat in 2025.
- Fangtooth fish (*Anoplogaster cornuta*) – Deep-sea specialist with huge teeth.

## Abyssal & Hadal (below 4000 m, trenches)
- Carnivorous “death-ball” sponges (*Chondrocladia*) – Covered in tiny hooks to catch prey.
- Zombie worms (*Osedax*) – No mouth; symbiotic bacteria digest whale bones.
- New iridescent scale worms, sea cucumbers (“Loki”), and a potential new branch of life (new amphipod superfamily *Mirabestioidea* from Clarion-Clipperton Zone).
- Snailfish filmed at record depths (>8000 m).

## Notes
The deep sea is the least explored habitat on Earth. Estimates suggest only ~10% of marine species have been described. Perfect for the “scending into darkness” part of the app.
