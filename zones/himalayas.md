# Himalayas – Rare Creatures by Altitude

## Extreme high (>5000–6700 m)
- **Himalayan jumping spider** (*Euophrys omnisuperstes*)  
  Tiny black jumping spider (~5 mm). Permanent resident up to ~6700 m on Everest and Makalu. Hunts wind-blown springtails and flies in rocky debris above the plant line. Name means “standing above everything”.

## High / alpine (2800–4500 m)
- **Ili pika** (*Ochotona iliensis*)  
  Extremely rare “magic rabbit”. Endemic to Tian Shan mountains (Xinjiang, China), 2800–4100 m. Round teddy-bear face, rounded ears, rusty-red patches on forehead and neck. Fewer than 1000 individuals estimated. Lives in talus slopes and rock crevices.
