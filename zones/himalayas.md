# Himalayas – Rare Creatures by Altitude (Expanded)

## Extreme high (>5000–6700 m)
- **Himalayan jumping spider** (*Euophrys omnisuperstes*)  
  Tiny black jumping spider (~5 mm). Permanent resident up to ~6700 m on Everest and Makalu. Hunts wind-blown springtails and flies.

- **Tardigrades and springtails** on high glaciers and rocky debris.

## High / alpine (2800–4500 m)
- **Ili pika** (*Ochotona iliensis*) – “Magic rabbit”. Extremely rare, Tian Shan / nearby high mountains, 2800–4100 m. Round face, rusty patches.

- **Snow leopard** (*Panthera uncia*) – Elusive, mostly 3000–5500 m. Still rare and hard to see.

- **Himalayan musk deer** (*Moschus chrysogaster* and relatives) – Rare, steep forests and alpine zones.

- **Western tragopan** and other rare pheasants of high forests.

## Montane forests (1500–2800 m)
- **Red panda** (*Ailurus fulgens*) – Eastern Himalayas, bamboo forests.

- Various rare moths and insects specialized to high-elevation forests.
