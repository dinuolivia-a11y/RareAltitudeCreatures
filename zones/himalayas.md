# Himalayas – Rare Creatures

Solo specie con nome scientifico preciso e documentazione affidabile.

1. **Himalayan jumping spider** (*Euophrys omnisuperstes*)  
   Tiny dark jumping spider (~4–5 mm). Permanent resident up to ~6700 m on Everest and Makalu. Feeds on wind-blown springtails and flies. Highest permanent animal resident known.

2. **Western woolly flying squirrel** (*Eupetaurus cinereus*)  
   One of the largest flying squirrels. Thick grizzled-grey woolly fur. Lives near treeline on steep cliffs in northern Pakistan and northwestern India. Thought extinct until rediscovered in the 1990s.

3. **Tibetan woolly flying squirrel** (*Eupetaurus tibetensis*)  
   Large flying squirrel of the north-central Himalayas (Tibet, Sikkim, Bhutan). Soft brown fur. Lives at high elevations near the treeline.

4. **Yunnan woolly flying squirrel** (*Eupetaurus nivamons*)  
   Large flying squirrel of northwestern Yunnan (China). Brownish woolly fur. Lives on steep alpine cliffs.

5. **Snow leopard** (*Panthera uncia*)  
   Elusive high-altitude cat of the Himalayas and Central Asia. Thick spotted coat, long tail. Mostly 3000–5500 m.

6. **Himalayan musk deer** (*Moschus chrysogaster*)  
   Small deer of steep alpine forests and grasslands. Males have a musk gland. Extremely elusive, threatened by poaching.

7. **Red panda** (*Ailurus fulgens*)  
   Arboreal specialist of temperate bamboo forests in the Eastern Himalayas. Reddish fur, ringed tail.
