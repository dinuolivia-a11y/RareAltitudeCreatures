# Himalayas – 10 Rare Creatures

1. **Himalayan jumping spider** (*Euophrys omnisuperstes*) – Tiny black jumping spider (~5 mm). Permanent resident up to ~6700 m on Everest and Makalu. Hunts wind-blown springtails and flies.
2. **Ili pika** (*Ochotona iliensis*) – Extremely rare “magic rabbit” of the Tian Shan / nearby high mountains (2800–4100 m). Round face, rusty patches.
3. **Snow leopard** (*Panthera uncia*) – Elusive high-altitude cat, mostly 3000–5500 m. Thick spotted coat.
4. **Himalayan musk deer** (*Moschus chrysogaster* and relatives) – Rare, steep forests and alpine zones. Known for the musk gland.
5. **Western tragopan** – Rare pheasant of high Himalayan forests.
6. **Red panda** (*Ailurus fulgens*) – Eastern Himalayas specialist of bamboo forests.
7. Tardigrades and springtails on high glaciers and rocky debris – Micro-fauna of extreme altitudes.
8. **Himalayan monal** – Colorful pheasant of high elevations, still uncommon.
9. Various rare high-elevation moths specialized to alpine and subalpine zones.
10. Specialized high-altitude beetles and ground arthropods of the Himalayan slopes.

## Notes
Extreme elevational gradient with both large rare mammals and tiny permanent residents of the highest peaks.
