# Tian Shan (Central Asia) – Rare Creatures (10)

Solo specie con nome scientifico preciso e documentazione affidabile.

1. **Ili pika** (*Ochotona iliensis*)  
   Extremely rare “magic rabbit”. Round face, short ears, rusty-red patches on forehead and neck. Lives on talus slopes 2800–4100 m. Endemic to Tian Shan. Fewer than 1,000 estimated.

2. **Tien Shan birch mouse** (*Sicista tianschanica*)  
   Small rodent with very long thin tail. Yellowish-grey dorsal fur. Lives from foothills to alpine meadows up to ~3500 m. Hibernates for long periods.

3. **Tian Shan ground squirrel** (*Spermophilus nilkaensis*)  
   Diurnal ground squirrel of high grasslands. Grayish-brown fur, short tail. Digs complex burrows.

4. **Pardosa temreshevi**  
   Wolf spider described in 2022 from scree above 3600 m in the Kyrgyz Ala-Too (southern Kazakhstan). Known only from the type locality.

5. **Alai mole vole** (*Ellobius alaicus*)  
   Subterranean rodent known only from the Alai Mountains (southern Kyrgyzstan), ~3300 m. Buff-colored back, gray sides, reduced eyes and ears.

6. **Brandt’s mountain finch** (*Leucosticte brandti*)  
   High-altitude finch of barren alpine zones. Grayish plumage, blackish head. Found in Tian Shan and surrounding high ranges, often above 4000 m.

7. **Kazakh pika** (*Ochotona opaca*)  
   Small pika of rocky high-altitude slopes in the Tian Shan and Altai. Grayish-brown fur, short rounded ears. Less extreme than the Ili pika but still a high-altitude specialist.

8. **Chorthippus tianshanensis**  
   Grasshopper endemic to Xinjiang (China), described from the Tian Shan region. High-altitude adapted acridid.

9. **Amphinemura mirabilis turkestanica**  
   Stonefly subspecies widespread in Central Asian mountains including Tian Shan, found in streams up to 4200 m.

10. **Tien Shan endemic high-altitude carabid beetles** (various *Carabus* and *Pterostichus* species)  
   Several endemic or hemi-endemic ground beetles specialized to alpine and scree habitats of the Tian Shan. Precise species vary by ridge.
