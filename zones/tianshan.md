# Tian Shan (Central Asia) – Rare Creatures (10)

## High altitude (2800–4100+ m)
1. **Ili pika** (*Ochotona iliensis*) – Extremely rare “magic rabbit”. Round teddy-bear face, rusty-red patches on forehead and neck. Lives in talus slopes. Fewer than 1000 estimated. Endemic to Tian Shan.
2. **Tien Shan birch mouse** (*Sicista tianschanica*) – Small rodent with long tail, yellowish-grey fur. Lives from piedmont to alpine meadows. Active only part of the year.
3. **Tian Shan ground squirrel** (*Spermophilus nilkaensis*) – Diurnal ground squirrel of high grasslands. Digs complex burrows and surveys surroundings upright.
4. **Pardosa temreshevi** – New high-altitude wolf spider species described from scree above 3600 m in southern Kazakhstan Tian Shan.

## Other specialists
5. Cryptic mole voles (*Ellobius alaicus* and relatives) – Rapidly evolving chromosomal forms in the Pamir-Alay and Tien Shan region.
6. High-altitude stoneflies (Plecoptera) – Several poorly known species recently recorded from Eastern Tian Shan ranges.
7. Endemic high-mountain plants with associated specialized insects (e.g. narrow-range Astragalus and Taraxacum species).
8. Alpine pikas and related lagomorphs of the region.
9. Rare high-elevation moths and micro-moths specialized to juniper and alpine shrubs.
10. Specialized beetles and ground-dwelling arthropods of talus and scree slopes.

## Notes
Tian Shan has high plant endemism and several rare small mammals and arthropods still being studied.
