# Tian Shan (Central Asia) – Rare Creatures

Solo specie con nome scientifico preciso e documentazione affidabile.

1. **Ili pika** (*Ochotona iliensis*)  
   Extremely rare “magic rabbit”. Round face, short ears, rusty-red patches on forehead and neck. Lives on talus slopes 2800–4100 m. Endemic to Tian Shan. Fewer than 1,000 estimated. Discovered 1983, rarely photographed.

2. **Tien Shan birch mouse** (*Sicista tianschanica*)  
   Small rodent with very long thin tail. Yellowish-grey dorsal fur, whitish underparts. Lives from foothills to alpine meadows up to ~3500 m. Hibernates for long periods. Found in China, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan.

3. **Tian Shan ground squirrel** (*Spermophilus nilkaensis*)  
   Diurnal ground squirrel of high grasslands. Grayish-brown fur, short tail. Digs complex burrows and often stands upright to survey surroundings. High-altitude specialist of the region.

4. **Pardosa temreshevi**  
   Wolf spider described in 2022 from scree above 3600 m in the Kyrgyz Ala-Too (southern Kazakhstan Tian Shan). Known only from the type locality. Increased number of ventral spines on tibiae I.

5. **Alai mole vole** (*Ellobius alaicus*)  
   Subterranean rodent known only from the Alai Mountains (southern Kyrgyzstan), around 3300 m. Buff-colored back, gray sides and belly, reduced eyes and ears. Distinguished mainly by chromosomal differences from related mole voles.
