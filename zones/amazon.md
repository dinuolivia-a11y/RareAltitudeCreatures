# Amazon – Rare Creatures

Solo specie con nome scientifico preciso e documentazione affidabile.

1. **Amazonian manatee** (*Trichechus inunguis*)  
   Freshwater manatee endemic to the Amazon basin. Smallest of the manatees.

2. **Giant otter** (*Pteronura brasiliensis*)  
   Endangered aquatic mammal of Amazonian rivers. Highly social and charismatic.

3. **White-cheeked spider monkey** (*Ateles marginatus*)  
   Endangered primate endemic to the Brazilian Amazon.

4. **Drypetes oliveri**  
   Giant tree (up to ~35 m) described in 2025 from Tambopata, Peru. Known from only a handful of individuals.

5. **Laimosemion laranja**  
   Tiny annual killifish described in 2026 from temporary pools in southern Amapá (Brazil). Extremely restricted range.

6. **Ipomoea cavalcantei**  
   Rare endemic plant of the iron-rich canga formations of Serra dos Carajás (Brazil).
