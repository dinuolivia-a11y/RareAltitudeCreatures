# Andes – 10 Rare Creatures

1. **Andean leaf-eared mouse** (*Phyllotis vaccarum*) – Highest-living mammal known. Live specimen captured on Llullaillaco summit at 6739 m. Large leaf-like ears, grayish-brown fur. Survives on lichens and wind-blown material.
2. **Andean mountain cat** (*Leopardus jacobita*) – One of the rarest small wild cats. Rocky habitats mostly above 4000 m. Thick grayish coat with spots, bushy tail. Extremely elusive.
3. **Liolaemus arequipensis** – Recently described high-Andean lizard living between ~3770–4800 m in the Peruvian Andes.
4. **Vicuña** (*Vicugna vicugna*) – High-altitude camelid specialist of the puna, adapted to thin air and cold.
5. New *Pristimantis* frogs from the Cordillera de Huancabamba cloud forests – Several recently described high-elevation endemics.
6. High-altitude springtails and mites on volcanic summits – Specialized micro-fauna living above 5000 m.
7. **Andean condor** (*Vultur gryphus*) – Iconic large soaring bird of the high Andes, still relatively rare in many areas.
8. **Spectacled bear** (*Tremarctos ornatus*) – Only bear species of South America, found in cloud forests and high Andean zones.
9. Various endemic high-elevation rodents (*Abrothrix*, *Akodon*, other *Phyllotis*) recorded above 5000 m.
10. Specialized high-Andean insects and beetles of the puna and rocky slopes.

## Notes
The Central Andes continue to reveal animals living higher than previously believed.
