# Andes – Rare Creatures by Altitude (Further Expanded)

## Extreme high (>5000–6700 m)
- **Andean leaf-eared mouse** (*Phyllotis vaccarum*) – Highest mammal. Live on Llullaillaco at 6739 m.
- Other *Phyllotis* species and high-altitude rodents recorded above 5000 m.
- High-altitude springtails and mites on volcanic summits.

## High altitude (3000–5000 m)
- **Andean mountain cat** (*Leopardus jacobita*) – Extremely rare, rocky habitats mostly >4000 m.
- **Liolaemus arequipensis** and other recently described high-Andean lizards (up to ~4800 m).
- **Vicuña** (*Vicugna vicugna*) – High-altitude specialist, though more known.
- New *Pristimantis* frogs from cloud forests of northern Andes.

## Subalpine / cloud forest (1500–3000 m)
- Various endemic frogs, birds and insects of the Andean cloud forests.

## Notes
The Andes have extreme elevational gradients. Many small mammals and reptiles still being recorded at higher elevations than previously known.
