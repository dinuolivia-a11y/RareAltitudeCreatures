# Andes – Rare Creatures (10)

Solo specie con nome scientifico preciso e documentazione affidabile.

1. **Andean leaf-eared mouse** (*Phyllotis vaccarum*)  
   Highest-living mammal known. Live specimen on Llullaillaco summit at 6739 m. Large leaf-like ears, grayish-brown fur.

2. **Andean mountain cat** (*Leopardus jacobita*)  
   One of the rarest small wild cats. Thick grayish coat with dark spots, bushy ringed tail. Rocky habitats mostly above 4000 m. Endangered.

3. **Liolaemus arequipensis**  
   High-Andean lizard described in 2026 from Arequipa (Peru). Lives 3770–4818 m. Small, viviparous, sexual dimorphism in pattern.

4. **Vicuña** (*Vicugna vicugna*)  
   High-altitude camelid of the puna. Slender, long neck, cinnamon back and white underparts.

5. **Spectacled bear** (*Tremarctos ornatus*)  
   Only bear of South America. Blackish-brown fur with light facial markings around the eyes.

6. **Andean condor** (*Vultur gryphus*)  
   One of the largest flying birds. Black body, white neck ruff, bare reddish head.

7. **Andean soft-haired mouse** (*Abrothrix andina*)  
   High-elevation rodent of the Altiplano, recorded above 5000 m in recent surveys. Small, grayish-brown fur.

8. **Jelski’s Altiplano mouse** (*Abrothrix jelskii*)  
   Medium-sized rodent of high Andean grasslands and rocky areas, from Peru to northern Argentina. Distinctive long silky fur.

9. **Altiplano laucha** (*Eligmodontia puerulus*)  
   Small rodent recorded at high elevations in the Central Andes during recent mountaineering surveys.

10. **High-Andean Pristimantis frogs** (recently described species from cloud forests and puna)  
   Several endemic high-elevation frogs of the genus Pristimantis described in recent years from Andean cloud forests and grasslands. Specific species vary by locality.
