# Andes – Rare Creatures by Altitude

## Extreme high (>5000–6700 m)
- **Andean leaf-eared mouse** (*Phyllotis vaccarum*)  
  Highest-living mammal known. Found alive on Llullaillaco volcano summit (~6739 m). Medium-sized mouse with large leaf-like ears, grayish-brown dorsal fur with ochre tones, whitish belly, bicolored tail. Survives on lichens and wind-blown seeds in near-barren rock and ice. Range also extends much lower.

## High altitude (3000–5000 m)
- **Andean mountain cat** (*Leopardus jacobita*)  
  One of the rarest small wild cats. Lives in rocky high-altitude habitats of the Andes (Argentina, Bolivia, Chile, Peru). Thick grayish coat with dark spots/rosettes, bushy tail. Extremely elusive; population estimates very low.
