# Andes – Rare Creatures

Solo specie con nome scientifico preciso e documentazione affidabile.

1. **Andean leaf-eared mouse** (*Phyllotis vaccarum*)  
   Highest-living mammal known. Live specimen captured on Llullaillaco summit at 6739 m. Large leaf-like ears, grayish-brown fur with ochre tones. Survives on lichens and wind-blown material in extreme conditions.

2. **Andean mountain cat** (*Leopardus jacobita*)  
   One of the rarest small wild cats. Thick grayish coat with dark spots and stripes, bushy ringed tail. Lives in rocky habitats mostly above 4000 m. Extremely elusive.

3. **Liolaemus arequipensis**  
   High-Andean lizard described in 2026 from Arequipa region (Peru). Lives between ~3770–4818 m in puna grasslands and rocky slopes. Small (6–8 cm), viviparous, with sexual dimorphism in pattern.

4. **Vicuña** (*Vicugna vicugna*)  
   High-altitude camelid of the puna. Slender body, long neck, cinnamon-brown back and white underparts. Perfectly adapted to thin air and cold above 3500–4000 m.

5. **Spectacled bear** (*Tremarctos ornatus*)  
   Only bear species of South America. Stocky body, blackish-brown fur with distinctive light facial markings around the eyes. Found in Andean cloud forests and high zones.

6. **Andean condor** (*Vultur gryphus*)  
   One of the largest flying birds. Black body with white neck ruff and white wing patches, bare reddish head. Soars over high Andean peaks.
