# Andes – Rare Creatures by Altitude (Expanded)

## Extreme high (>5000–6700 m)
- **Andean leaf-eared mouse** (*Phyllotis vaccarum*)  
  Highest-living mammal known. Live specimen captured on Llullaillaco summit at 6739 m. Medium-sized mouse with large leaf-like ears, grayish-brown fur with ochre tones, whitish belly. Survives on lichens and wind-blown material where almost nothing grows. Has the widest elevational range of any mammal (sea level to >6700 m).

- Other high-altitude *Phyllotis* and related rodents recorded above 5000 m on multiple volcanoes.

## High altitude (3000–5000 m)
- **Andean mountain cat** (*Leopardus jacobita*)  
  One of the rarest small wild cats. Rocky habitats mostly above 4000 m in Peru, Bolivia, Chile, Argentina. Thick grayish coat with spots, bushy tail. Extremely elusive; very low density.

- **Liolaemus arequipensis** and other high-Andean lizards – Recently described species living up to ~4800 m in Peruvian Andes.

- New *Pristimantis* frogs from Cordillera de Huancabamba cloud forests (high-elevation endemics).

## Notes
The Central Andes continue to reveal that mammals and reptiles can live far higher than previously believed. Many invertebrates and plants remain poorly studied at extreme elevations.
