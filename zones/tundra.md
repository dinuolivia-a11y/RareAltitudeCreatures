# Tundra – Rare Creatures (Arctic & Alpine)

## Extreme / recently discovered
- **Paramacrobiotus klimaszyki** – New tardigrade (“water bear”) species described from leaf litter on Svalbard. Transparent, eyeless. First new species found in deciduous litter there.
- **Glacier springtails** (genus *Desoria* and others) – Live directly on ice surfaces of high mountains and polar regions. Feed on cold-loving algae and fungi. Many still undescribed.
- **Arctic bumblebee** (*Bombus polaris*) – One of the few bees that can fly and forage in near-freezing Arctic temperatures.

## Specialized but slightly more known
- **Collared lemming** (*Dicrostonyx groenlandicus*) – Turns white in winter, digs under snow. Classic Arctic specialist.
- **Arctic fox** (*Vulpes lagopus*) – Iconic but populations in some areas are rare and climate-threatened.
- **Rock ptarmigan** (*Lagopus muta*) – Perfectly camouflaged year-round in alpine and Arctic tundra.

## Notes
Tundra and polar regions still yield new microscopic and invertebrate species regularly.
