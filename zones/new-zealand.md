# New Zealand – Rare Creatures

Solo specie con nome scientifico preciso e documentazione affidabile. Quasi tutte endemiche.

1. **Kākāpō** (*Strigops habroptilus*)  
   World’s only flightless parrot. Nocturnal, critically endangered. Fewer than 250 individuals on predator-free islands.

2. **Kea** (*Nestor notabilis*)  
   World’s only alpine parrot. Intelligent, curious, lives in the Southern Alps.

3. **North Island brown kiwi** (*Apteryx mantelli*)  
   Iconic flightless bird with long bill. Nocturnal, endemic to New Zealand.

4. **Takahē** (*Porphyrio hochstetteri*)  
   Large flightless rail, once thought extinct. Rediscovered and carefully managed.

5. **Black stilt** (*Himantopus novaezelandiae*)  
   One of the rarest wading birds in the world. Black plumage, long pink legs. Critically endangered.

6. **Black robin** (*Petroica traversi*)  
   Once reduced to five birds. Famous conservation recovery on the Chatham Islands.

7. **Lesser short-tailed bat** (*Mystacina tuberculata*)  
   One of New Zealand’s only native land mammals. Unique ancient bat lineage. Spends much time on the ground.

8. **Hector’s dolphin** (*Cephalorhynchus hectori*)  
   Smallest and rarest marine dolphin. Endemic to New Zealand coastal waters.

9. **Tuatara** (*Sphenodon punctatus*)  
   Living fossil, the only surviving member of its order. Found only on offshore islands.

10. **New Zealand sea lion** (*Phocarctos hookeri*)  
   Endemic sea lion, one of the rarest in the world. Breeds mainly on subantarctic islands.

---

*Tutte le specie sopra sono precise, documentate e quasi tutte endemiche della Nuova Zelanda.*
