# Appalachians – Rare Creatures

Solo specie con nome scientifico preciso e documentazione affidabile.

1. **Fraser fir** (*Abies fraseri*)  
   Conifer endemic to the highest elevations of the Southern Appalachians. Restricted to a few mountain tops.

2. **Spruce-fir moss spider** (*Microhexura montivaga*)  
   Tiny endangered spider that lives only in high-elevation spruce-fir forests of the Southern Appalachians.

3. **Carolina northern flying squirrel** (*Glaucomys sabrinus coloratus*)  
   Extremely rare subspecies of flying squirrel restricted to the highest ridges of North Carolina, Tennessee and Virginia.

4. **Jordan’s salamander** (*Plethodon jordani*)  
   Endemic salamander of the Great Smoky Mountains with distinctive red cheeks.

5. **Rock gnome lichen** (*Cetradonia linearis*)  
   Rare lichen endemic to high-elevation rock faces in the Southern Appalachians.

6. **Weller’s salamander** (*Plethodon welleri*)  
   Small endemic salamander of high-elevation spruce-fir forests in the Southern Appalachians.
