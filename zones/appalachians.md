# Appalachians – Rare High-Elevation Creatures (Expanded)

## High ridges / spruce-fir zone (>1500 m)
- **Spruce-fir moss spider** (*Microhexura montivaga*) – Tiny tarantula relative (3–4 mm). Only in damp moss under Fraser firs on a few peaks. Critically endangered.
- **Carolina northern flying squirrel** (*Glaucomys sabrinus coloratus*) – Extremely rare subspecies. Fewer than 500 estimated. High ridgelines only.
- **Appalachian cottontail** (*Sylvilagus obscurus*) – Specialized to high rocky ridgelines. Looks similar to common cottontail but genetically distinct.
- **Boreal owl** and **three-toed woodpecker** – Rare birds closely tied to old-growth high spruce-fir forests.

## Notes
High-elevation Appalachian endemics are often restricted to shrinking spruce-fir islands.
