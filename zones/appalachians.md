# Appalachians – Rare High-Elevation Creatures

## High ridges / spruce-fir zone (>1500 m)
- **Spruce-fir moss spider** (*Microhexura montivaga*)  
  Tiny tarantula relative (3–4 mm). Lives only in damp moss mats under Fraser firs on a few mountaintops (Grandfather Mountain, Mount Mitchell, Roan Mountain). Builds tube webs. Critically endangered due to fir die-off.

- **Carolina northern flying squirrel** (*Glaucomys sabrinus coloratus*)  
  Extremely rare subspecies. Fewer than 500 individuals estimated. Lives only on highest ridgelines of North Carolina, Tennessee, Virginia in old-growth spruce-fir forests. Glides between trees.
