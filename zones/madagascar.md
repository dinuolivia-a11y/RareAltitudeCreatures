# Madagascar – Rare & Endemic Creatures

Madagascar has one of the highest rates of endemism on Earth. Many species are micro-endemic (found only in tiny areas) and still being discovered.

## Recently rediscovered or newly described (most obscure)
- **Spirostreptus sculptus** – Giant millipede lost to science since 1897, rediscovered in Makira Forest in 2023.
- **Brookesia nofy** – Tiny leaf chameleon (~33 mm) discovered thanks to tourists. Critically endangered.
- **Paragehyra tsaranoro** – New gecko found only in three tiny forest fragments in the Andringitra Massif. Micro-endemic and critically endangered.
- **Rhombophryne** diamond frogs – Seven new species described in 2026. Secretive, mostly brown with hidden bright markings. Many more likely exist.
- **Kynotus insularis** – New giant earthworm (~80 cm) from Nosy Be, belonging to Madagascar’s endemic earthworm family Kynotidae.
- **Voeltzkowia volontany** and **V. shai-hulud** – Two new worm-like burrowing lizards discovered in northern sands. Already critically endangered.

## Classic but still rare endemics
- **Aye-aye** (*Daubentonia madagascariensis*) – Unique nocturnal lemur with elongated finger. Extremely rare and culturally feared.
- **Fossa** (*Cryptoprocta ferox*) – Madagascar’s largest native predator. Elusive and declining.

## Notes
Madagascar continues to yield dozens of new species every year, especially amphibians, reptiles and invertebrates.
